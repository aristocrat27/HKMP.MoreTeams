Supports adding outlines for custom teams, customizing text and outline colors, as well as the ability to disable outlines entirely.

# Integration with HKMP.List:
* Allows configuring leaders for custom teams and MoreTeams.
* Configuring team creation permissions for players (host only, and admins with the corresponding permission).
* Configuring whether the player who created the team automatically becomes its leader (host only, and admins with the corresponding permission).
* Ability to choose a skin for the team and lock it for all players (host, team leader, and admins with the corresponding permission only).
